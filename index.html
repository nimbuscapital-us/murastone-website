About Murastone

Flexible stone for modern architecture

Murastone distributes Magic Stone flexible panels — pure soft stone surfaces made by pressing natural stone powder. The material combines super toughness with a lightweight profile, allowing flat and curved installations including columns as small as 20 mm in diameter without cracking.

Inspired by mountains, desert landscapes, ocean tones, and organic textures, our catalog spans travertine, marble, cement, wood, terrazzo, and rammed earth finishes for residential, hospitality, retail, and commercial projects.

  • Rich surface textures that replicate natural materials
  • Standard sizes up to 3000 × 1200 mm
  • Ideal for walls, background walls, and landscape features
  • Suitable for modern minimalist and classical luxury styles
